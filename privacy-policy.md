# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2025_

**TodoList** is a personal productivity app built with SwiftUI.

We respect your privacy.  
This application **does not collect, store, or share any personal information**.

All your data, including tasks and settings, are stored **locally on your device** using Apple’s secure storage system (UserDefaults).  
No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are used.
